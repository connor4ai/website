Mokara Spa

Cedar, limestone and warm stone — a spa drawn from the land itself.

Sixteen treatment rooms, a private adults-only spa pool deck, sauna and cold plunge — with rituals built around Hill Country botanicals and the quiet of the canyon.

Hill Country Stone Ritual90 min Warm limestone and cedar oil press the week out of your shoulders, one slow pass at a time. The signature treatment, and rightly so. Reserve Wildflower Glow Facial60 min Prickly pear, honey and spring water — a facial that borrows everything from the meadow and gives it all back to your skin. Reserve Creekside Sound Bath45 min Expert-led mindfulness beneath the oaks — bowls, breath and birdsong, ending in the kind of quiet you can keep. Reserve
