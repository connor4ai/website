Nine tables, one long appetite

From prime steakhouse fare to sunset cocktails on the canyon rim — dining is the second sport here.

The kitchens

Wood smoke, spring water and whatever the garden gave us this morning.

No. 01 Bob's Steak & Chop House Steakhouse · Dinner Prime cuts, glazed carrots the size of Texas and a wine list as deep as the canyon. The classic, done the Austin way. Reserve a table No. 02 Blind Salamander Kitchen & Bar New Texan · All day Named for the rarest resident of the Edwards Aquifer — wood-fired Hill Country plates with long canyon views. Reserve a table No. 03 The Overlook Terrace Cocktails · Sunset Frozen ranch waters and mesquite-smoked snacks on the canyon rim. The best golden hour in Travis County. Reserve a table No. 04 Verbena Poolside Grill · Poolside Smashburgers, paletas and spiked agua frescas delivered to your cabana, dripping-wet guests welcome. Reserve a table No. 05 Cedar & Sage Coffee Co. Café & market · Morning Hill Country lattes, kolaches before the first tee and picnic boxes packed for the trail. Reserve a table No. 06 The Palm Lounge Lobby bar · Evening Low light, live piano on weekends and a mezcal list curated like a library. Reserve a table No. 07 The Turn House Grill 19th hole · Lunch Brisket tacos and cold Shiner between nines — scorecards argued at the rail. Reserve a table No. 08 Firelight S'mores & sips · Night Fire pits, toasting forks and a nightcap cart under more stars than you remembered existing. Reserve a table No. 09 The Chef's Garden Table Tasting · Seasonal Eight seats inside the kitchen garden — one menu, picked the same afternoon it's served. Reserve a table
