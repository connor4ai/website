Barton Creek · Austin, TX

Our story

Some places you visit. This one, you return to — season after season, generation after generation.

Set on four thousand acres where the Edwards Plateau folds into canyon and creek, Barton Creek has spent four decades perfecting one idea: that Texas hospitality and wild country belong together.

Four decades on the creek

A brief history

1986

The gates open

Fazio Foothills and Palmer Lakeside debut, and a quiet stretch of canyon outside Austin becomes Texas golf's new address.

1991

Crenshaw comes home

Austin's own Ben Crenshaw and Bill Coore add a classical, walkable third course on the property's southern meadows.

2000

The Canyons

Tom Fazio returns to carve a second masterpiece along Short Springs Branch — wide, wild and instantly beloved.

2019

The reimagining

A $150 million renovation rethinks all 493 rooms, the spa and every table — Hill Country modern, top to bottom.

2026

The next chapter

The Residences open their books, inviting a fortunate few to make the view permanent.

Stewardship

The land comes first
